# Home Cooking Statistics Research - Compiled Data

## Overview
Research compiled for the blog article: "The State of Home Cooking: 2025 Statistics & Trends"

---

## 1. How Many Americans Cook at Home

### Current Statistics (2024-2025)
- **81%** of consumers cook more than half of their meals at home (NFRA Eat at Home Report)
- **93%** of Americans expect to cook as much or more in the next 12 months (HelloFresh 2025 Report)
- **61%** of Americans cook a meal at home on Mondays, compared to 49% on Fridays
- **64%** of Americans continue cooking at home to save money and control budget

### Cooking Frequency
- Average home-cooked meals dropped from **10.9 to 10 per week** between 2018 and 2022
- U.S. specifically reached a "historic low" at **8.2 meals per week** in 2022 compared to 9.4 in 2020
- **69%** of adults have sit-down dinners at home with others most or every day

### Barriers to Cooking
- **1 in 5** adults skip cooking after work because they're too exhausted
- **20%** cite lack of time as biggest barrier
- **19%** cite after-work fatigue
- **86%** are "meal repeaters" eating same meals repeatedly

## 2. Time Spent Cooking by Generation

### Cooking Frequency by Generation
| Generation | Days Cooking Per Week | Cook Daily | Cook 4+ Times/Week |
|------------|----------------------|------------|-------------------|
| Gen Z | 5.9 days | 11% | 52% |
| Millennials | 6.7 days | - | - |
| Boomers | - | 26% | - |

### Weekend Cooking Habits
- **70%** of Baby Boomers cook dinner from scratch every Sunday
- **40%** of Gen Z cook dinner from scratch every Sunday

### Gen Z Cooking Attitudes
- **51%** of Gen Z say they "like" or "love" meal preparation (vs 46% overall)
- **33%** of Gen Z consider themselves skilled cooks (vs 47% of Millennials)
- **49%** of Gen Z hesitant to cook for others due to lack of skills
- Gen Z are **30% more likely** to consume frozen/microwaveable meals than Millennials

### Millennial Habits
- **76%** of Millennials have sit-down dinners with others most days (highest of all generations)
- **50%** of Millennials use meal shortcuts weekly (highest of all generations)

## 3. Why People Cook at Home

### Top Motivations
| Reason | Percentage |
|--------|------------|
| Save money | 35% |
| Healthier than takeout/dining out | 89% |
| Best way to save on food | 89% |
| Nutrition | 26% |

### Health Focus
- **81%** cite health as reason they'll cook more in next 12 months
- **57%** of Gen Z prefer cooking at home because it's healthier
- **63%** of Millennials prefer cooking at home because it's healthier

### Economic Pressure (2025)
- **83%** say saving money is bigger priority in 2025 than other years
- **81%** say saving money on food is a priority this year
- **78%** eating at home more to save money amid rising costs
- **68%** didn't buy a food item in past 12 months due to high price
- **67%** say rising prices made it harder to buy healthy food

## 4. Cost: Home Cooking vs Eating Out

### Average Cost Per Meal
| Meal Type | Average Cost |
|-----------|-------------|
| Home-cooked meal | $4.00 - $6.00 |
| Home meal (specific) | $4.23 |
| Inexpensive restaurant | $15.00 - $20.00+ |
| Restaurant meal (specific) | $16.00+ |

### Savings
- Americans save around **$12 per meal** by cooking at home
- Annual cost difference: Over **$13,000** more to eat out
- Restaurant meals cost **2x to 3x** more than homemade
- Delivery from restaurants is **5x more expensive** than cooking
- Meal kits are **3x more expensive** than cooking from scratch

### Price Inflation (2024)
- Food at home rose **1.2%** (USDA)
- Food away from home rose **4.1%** (USDA)
- Nov 2023-Nov 2024: Food away from home up **3.6%**, food at home up **1.6%** (CPI)

### Regional Variations
- New York: Save **$19.40 per meal** by cooking at home
- Expensive states (NY, CT, WA): At least **$15 savings** per meal

## 7. Post-Pandemic Cooking Trends

### During Pandemic (2020-2021)
- **60%** of Americans cooked at home more
- **44%** cooked more often since COVID start
- **25%** cooked more with family members
- **50-78%** reported greater cooking frequency in surveys

### Return to Pre-Pandemic
- North America: **8.4 meals per week** in 2022 (same as 2019)
- US: **8.2 meals per week** - "historic low" vs 9.4 in 2020
- Cooking at home decreased from pandemic peak but 64% continue for savings

### 2025 Outlook
- 5 years post-pandemic, mealtime continues evolving
- Influenced by: wellness trends, technology, social media, economic pressures
- 93% expect to maintain or increase home cooking
