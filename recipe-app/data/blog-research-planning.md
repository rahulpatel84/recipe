# Blog Research Planning - Linkable Content Strategy

## Goal
Create data-driven, research-backed blog content that attracts backlinks and references from other websites.

---

## Topic Ideas

### 1. The State of Home Cooking: 2025 Statistics & Trends ⭐ (RESEARCHING)
- How many people cook at home vs eat out
- Time spent cooking by generation (Gen Z, Millennials, Boomers)
- Most popular cuisines cooked at home
- Impact of social media on cooking habits
- **Backlink potential**: Food blogs, lifestyle sites, news outlets

### 2. Plant-Based Eating: The Numbers Behind the Movement
- Global vegan/vegetarian population growth
- Market size and projections
- Health outcomes data
- Environmental impact statistics
- **Backlink potential**: Health sites, environmental blogs, nutrition experts

### 3. The True Cost: Home Cooking vs Eating Out
- Average cost per meal comparison
- Annual savings from cooking at home
- Time investment analysis
- Regional cost differences
- **Backlink potential**: Personal finance blogs, frugal living sites, family blogs

### 4. Food Waste Statistics: A Global Crisis in Numbers
- Household food waste data
- Economic impact
- Environmental consequences
- Tips to reduce waste
- **Backlink potential**: Environmental sites, news outlets, sustainability blogs

### 5. The Psychology of Comfort Food: What Science Says
- Research on emotional eating
- Most popular comfort foods by country
- Nostalgia and food connection
- Stress eating statistics
- **Backlink potential**: Psychology sites, wellness blogs, academic references

---

## Research Sources to Check
- USDA / FDA reports
- Pew Research Center
- Statista
- Nielsen data
- Academic journals (PubMed, Google Scholar)
- Industry reports (Food Industry Association, NRA)
- Bureau of Labor Statistics
- WHO / FAO data

---

## Content Format for Maximum Linkability
1. Clear statistics with sources cited
2. Original data visualizations / infographics
3. Quotable statistics (easy to reference)
4. Annual update potential (evergreen)
5. Downloadable resources
6. Expert quotes if possible
