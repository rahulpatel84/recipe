Spiced Lentil Soup

★★★★★4.8 · 912 ratings · 214 cooks left notes

Red lentils melt into a silky, golden soup with cumin, smoked paprika and a bright hit of lemon. Pantry ingredients, one pot, and it tastes even better the next day.

Ingredients

    Method

    Cook mode (screen stays on)
    1

    Soften the aromatics

    Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot and a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until soft and just golden.

    2

    Bloom the spices

    Stir in the garlic, cumin, paprika and turmeric. Cook for one minute, until it smells amazing — don't let the garlic brown.

    3

    Simmer

    Add the rinsed lentils, tomatoes and stock. Bring to a boil, then lower to a gentle simmer, partly covered, until the lentils have completely broken down.

    Pip's tip

    Want it silky? Blend half the soup and stir it back in. You keep some texture and get a creamy body with zero cream.

    4

    Finish bright

    Take off the heat. Stir in the lemon juice, taste, and season with salt and pepper. Thin with a splash of water if it's too thick.

    5

    Serve

    Ladle into bowls, drizzle with olive oil, and top with chopped parsley and a pinch of chilli flakes. Warm flatbread on the side is non-negotiable.
